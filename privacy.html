Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information The Inclusion Exchange collects through this website, why, and what your rights are. We've kept it short and in plain language. If anything is unclear, just ask us.

Who we are

This website is run by Elizabeth Cameron, trading as The Inclusion Exchange. She is the controller of the personal information described here. You can contact us about anything in this notice at elizabeth@theinclusionexchange.uk.

What we collect, and why

When you use our contact form, we receive the details you choose to give us:

  • your name and email address (required, so we can reply)
  • your organisation, if you add it
  • the topic you're interested in and your message
  • how you'd prefer us to get back to you
  • anything you tell us that would make communicating with you easier, if you choose to fill this in

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and to follow up on it. We don't sell your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list from this form.

About the "communicating with us" question: this asks about your needs (for example, written follow-up or an agenda in advance). You never need to tell us about a diagnosis or a health condition. If you do choose to share something like that, we'll use it only to adjust how we communicate with you, and we rely on your explicit consent, which you give by choosing to include it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Our legal basis for using your contact details is our legitimate interest in responding to people who get in touch with us, and taking steps you've asked for before any agreement with you.

Who else handles your information

  • Formspree delivers messages from our contact form to our inbox. It handles them under its own privacy policy and data processing terms.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS WorkMail) hosts our email, which our web partner Groove Inc manages for us. The messages we receive are stored there.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) hosts this website. Like any web host, it processes your IP address to deliver pages and may keep it in server logs for security purposes.
  • Google Fonts: our pages load their typefaces from Google's servers, which means your browser shares your IP address with Google when you visit.

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States. Where your information is transferred outside the UK, we rely on the safeguards set out in each provider's data protection terms, as UK data protection law requires.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages for 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them, unless the enquiry leads to an ongoing working relationship. In that case we keep what we need for that relationship.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • ask us to restrict or stop using it, or object to how we use it
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email elizabeth@theinclusionexchange.uk. We'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use personal information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
